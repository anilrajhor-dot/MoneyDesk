MoneyDesk v4.0 — Help

A quick guide to the main features.

Dashboard

Shows spending, loans, tax estimate, goals, net worth and financial-health observations.

Expenses

Record, edit, filter and analyse expenses. Budgets and recurring expenses are supported.

Net Worth

Enter current asset values and outstanding liabilities to calculate net worth.

Loans & EMI

Calculate EMI, view amortisation and test prepayments.

Tax

Compare old and new tax regimes. Tax figures are maintained by financial year.

Goals

Plan SIPs, lumpsums, major goals and retirement needs.

Backup

Use “Back up now” regularly. Your data stays in this browser unless you export it.

Tax calculations are estimates for planning. Verify the applicable provisions before filing or making a tax decision.
